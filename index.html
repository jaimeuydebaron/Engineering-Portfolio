I'm a final-year Mechatronics Engineering student at the University of Canterbury with a passion for the biomedical industry — a space where engineering excellence directly improves people's lives. I've worked across orthopaedic implant design at Zimmer Biomet, cancer research at the University of Otago, and equity markets at Forsyth Barr, bringing the same approach to every environment: rigorous, documented, and outcome-driven.

Outside of engineering, I lead student organisations, run ultramarathons for charity, and coordinate disaster relief efforts. I'm at my best in fast-moving environments where the stakes are real.
